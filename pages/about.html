About me

Geoinformatics engineer, based in Dehradun.

02.1

My story

I've been doing geospatial research for more than four years, including work at DRDO and at Survey of India's Geodetic & Research Branch. Most of that work is about taking raw spatial data and automating the steps between the data and a decision.

At Survey of India I manage two wings. The NSRF Wing processes GNSS CORS data from 1023+ reference stations, and the HPL Wing maintains India's national vertical datum. My scripts brought GNSS processing down from 7 days to 3 days per station, and my levelling adjustment software made HPL computation about 100 times faster. QA/QC checks such as the Tau test and global statistical validation run inside the scripts, so manual errors don't get through.

At DRDO's Defence Geoinformatics Research Establishment I processed about 3 million LiDAR points and 32 hyperspectral bands. I used them to build flood inundation models and terrain vulnerability assessments for defence planning and disaster preparedness.

I also started the Geospatial Technology Club at NSUT. It grew to 200+ members from across India, and we brought in industry professionals for talks and workshops.

I try to write code that someone else can pick up later: clean, documented, and built to keep working as the data grows. The work I enjoy most is research where the numbers have to be right.
