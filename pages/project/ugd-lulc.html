01

Overview

A deep learning pipeline, now over 1 lakh lines of code, that classifies land use and land cover across India. It's trained on EuroSAT and reaches 98.1% validation accuracy. The pipeline runs in 6 phases, and labelling is done inside QGIS.

Four products are planned on top of it: GreenWatch (encroachment alerts), VillageLens (land use stats for each revenue village), CarbonSight (carbon credit estimates) and ReportGen (automatic bilingual MoEFCC/EIA reports).
