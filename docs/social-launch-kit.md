I am Aditya Kumar Jha, a GIS engineer in Dehradun working at the intersection of geodesy and software. My work includes GNSS CORS processing with GAMIT/GLOBK and PRIDE PPP-AR, high precision levelling, and Python automation for geospatial workflows.
